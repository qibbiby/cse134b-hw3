Race-Day Execution Plan

  1. Carb-Loading: Final meal 2-3 hours prior to gun time.
  2. Hydration: Electrolyte before race, sip water every 3km.
  3. Gels: Take energy gels every 3-4km
  4. Pacing Strategy: Start at race pace or slower, do not go faster than goal pace
  5. Negative Split Finish: Gradually go faster. Go all out at final 1-2kms
  6. Most Important!: Have fun on race day and enjoy the atmosphere :)
